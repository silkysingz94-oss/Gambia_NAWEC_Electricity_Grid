Methodology and limitations

Data are retrieved live from OpenStreetMap through Overpass API. The application queries separate categories of power infrastructure and combines successful results.

Line lengths are calculated from returned geometry. Capacity is included only when an interpretable capacity tag is available.

This is not a complete official NAWEC asset register, live SCADA feed, outage map, or engineering survey. An unmapped feature is not proof that infrastructure does not exist.
